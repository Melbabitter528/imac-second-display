## 🔧 Troubleshooting (Common Fixes)

**Problem: My iMac isn't showing up.**
- Make sure both devices are on the **same network** (same Wi-Fi name or same router).
- Restart both computers and try again.
- Temporarily turn off your firewall or antivirus, then test.

**Problem: The screen is blurry or laggy.**
- Move closer to your Wi-Fi router, or use Ethernet cables.
- Close other heavy programs on both computers.
- Check that your iMac is running OS X 10.9 or newer.

**Problem: The app won't open on Windows.**
- Right-click the app icon and choose "Run as administrator."
- Make sure you have the latest Windows updates installed.

## 📖 How It Works (Simple Explanation)

Your Windows computer creates a virtual second monitor. It takes whatever appears on that virtual screen and compresses it using a video format (H.264) that your iMac's hardware can decode instantly. Then it sends that compressed video over your network using a fast protocol (RTP/UDP). Your iMac receives it, decodes it, and displays it on its screen – all in milliseconds. It's like a super-fast YouTube video, but just for your desktop.
